SNOOPY FLYING ACE / RedBaron_Recomp - PC CONTROLS

Use the FlyingAce preset. Other native presets change button functions.
Mouse movement does not steer or move the camera.

MENUS
WASD navigates; Space confirms; Backspace goes back. Esc sends Start.
F4 opens runtime configuration; do not assign it to a gameplay action.

CUSTOMIZATION
Use the launcher Controls tab. Advanced bindings can be edited with the game
closed in snoopyflyingace.toml. Comma alternatives such as "LMB,J" accept either.
Mouse buttons: LMB/RMB/MMB; wheel/side buttons are not mapped. Avoid modifier
chords for simultaneous flight inputs. Keep mnk_mouse=false. mnk_mode=false
disables keyboard routing.

LOCAL PLAY
SDL maps supported controllers to Xbox positions. Xbox Series is the physical
reference. Other controllers/adapters need testing. Community mappings retain
their accompanying zlib license.

Keyboard/mouse share player 1 by default. For controller plus keyboard local
play, select Exclusive player 2 for keyboard, controller on player 1. Save and
restart before joining. Four input profiles do not create four cameras: native
local gameplay currently supports two. Avoid virtual controller duplicates.
